    How to read it. The amount is what the SBA approved, not what has been spent. Drawn is the SBA's first-disbursement date; a loan not yet drawn is committed money the borrower has not pulled. Likely buying is read off the borrower's NAICS code: a restaurant buys a kitchen and a hood, a clinic buys a fit-out, a manufacturer buys a line. Why now is the SBA's business-age field: opening, under two years old, changed hands, or existing.
